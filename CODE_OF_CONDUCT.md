# Code of Conduct

We want a professional and welcoming environment for contributors and users.

Be respectful, focus on technical evidence, give constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or sharing private information without permission. When reviewing security research, do not publicize secrets or exploit details from third-party projects.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately through their GitHub profile.
