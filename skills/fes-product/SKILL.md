# Product discovery & acceptance


Determine the real user outcome, primary use case, non-goals, constraints, and target deliverable.
For greenfield products propose an MVP and defer nice-to-haves. Validate costly assumptions with small experiments.
Produce 3–8 observable acceptance criteria with happy, negative and permission paths where relevant.
Record unresolved decisions separately. Avoid requirement bloat and don't hold harmless work hostage to questions.
Map every acceptance criterion to an implementation owner and later to verification evidence.
